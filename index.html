    Adaptive Skill Loop

    QA Bug Reporting · Business English B1–B2

    From isolated feedback to a continuous skill loop.

    Design question

    How might recurring language patterns from job-specific practice become targeted learning priorities — and then be checked again in a new work scenario?

    One-off feedback helps a learner correct the moment. This concept explores how repeated patterns could become visible learning priorities, trigger short targeted practice, and be reassessed in context.
